How It Works

1. Calibration: Measures environment audio reference levels to establish precision baseline.

2. Pure-Tone Audiometry: Tests thresholds across 500Hz, 1000Hz, 4000Hz, and 8000Hz (Presbycusis assessment).

3. Population Norm Comparison: Evaluates your thresholds against standard ISO 7029 age & gender benchmarks.

4. Future Deterioration Forecasting: Projects your personal hearing loss trajectory and calculates predicted age of impairment.
