How the Test Works

1. The test will start from complete silence (-70 dB HL) and gradually increase the sound signal.

2. Listen carefully for 3 short pulsed beeps at each frequency step.

3. As soon as you hear the beeps, immediately click "I Hear It".

4. The test evaluates low, mid, high, and very high (8000 Hz) pitches to measure age-related sensitivity.

5. Please use headphones in a quiet room for best accuracy.
